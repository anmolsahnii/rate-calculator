RATE CALCULATOR FOR GMAIL

1. Open chrome://extensions in Google Chrome.
2. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
3. Click Load unpacked.
4. Select the rate-calculator-chrome folder containing this file.
5. Pin Rate Calculator for Gmail to the Chrome toolbar.
6. Refresh Gmail once.

Open a customer quote email and click the gold Rate Calculator button.

Complete emails open the calculated quote automatically. If pickup,
destination, or pallet count is unclear, the popup lets you correct it first.
